Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{UPDATED_DATE}}

{{SITE_NAME}} shows the live status of popular AI tools. We want this site to be simple and respectful of your privacy. This page explains what information is used when you visit.

No accounts, no personal profile

You do not need to sign up to use this site. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number to view the status board.

Your watchlist stays on your device

When you star a tool, the list of starred tools is saved only in your own browser (in "local storage"). It is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it. If you use "Copy link to my watchlist", the tool names are placed in the link itself, and anyone you share that link with can see which tools are on it.

Tool requests

If you request a new tool, you are taken to a Google Form. Whatever you type there is collected by Google Forms on our behalf and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. Please don't enter anything there you don't want us to see.

Advertising and cookies

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can turn off personalised ads in Google My Ad Center. Visitors in some regions (such as the European Economic Area and the UK) are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Visit statistics

We may use Google Analytics to understand, in total, how many people visit and which pages are popular. This uses cookies and does not tell us who you are.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you load a page, for security and to keep the service running.

Links to other websites

The status board links to the websites of the tools listed. We are not responsible for how those websites handle your data.

Children

This site is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.
