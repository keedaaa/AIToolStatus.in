How {{SITE_NAME}} checks if an AI tool is down

About every 10 minutes, our checker visits the website of every tool listed above from the cloud and measures how quickly it answers. Where a company publishes an official status page, we also read it, so you see both our own check and what the company itself is reporting.

To avoid false alarms, a tool is only marked Down after it fails two checks in a row. A single failed check, a very slow answer, or a partial problem reported by the company shows as Issues. The 24-hour uptime figure is the share of our checks in the last day that did not find the tool down.
